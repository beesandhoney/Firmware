EC guidance (µS/cm)

(Approximate values at ~25 °C. Start low and increase gradually.)
  • Seedlings / clones: 300–700
  • Herbs (basil, parsley): 800–1400
  • Leafy greens (lettuce, spinach): 900–1600
  • Chili / pepper — Vegetative: 1000–1500; Flowering/Fruiting: 1500–2200
  • Fruiting plants (tomato, cucumber) — Vegetative: 1500–2200; Flowering/Fruiting: 2200–3500
  • Strawberry — Vegetative: 900–1400; Flowering/Fruiting: 1200–1800
  • Ornamental flowering plants — Vegetative: 1000–1500; Flowering: 1400–2000
Tip: Leaf burn → EC too high. Pale growth → EC too low.

Ambient light control

Phototransistor on GPIO34 dims or turns lights off when ambient light is high.
